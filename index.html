Our Story

Texas Biryani House is a cloud kitchen born from a passion for authentic Indian cuisine and a desire to share the rich flavors of traditional biryani with the DFW area.

Our chefs bring decades of experience from the kitchens of Hyderabad, known as the biryani capital of India. Every dish is prepared using time-honored recipes passed down through generations, combined with the freshest local ingredients.

As a cloud kitchen specializing in home delivery, we bring restaurant-quality biryani directly to your doorstep. We believe in the perfect balance of spices, the finest quality meats and vegetables, and cooking techniques that bring out the best in every grain of rice.
