Our Story

Texas Biryani House was born from a passion for authentic Indian cuisine and a desire to share the rich flavors of traditional biryani with the great state of Texas.

Our chefs bring decades of experience from the kitchens of Hyderabad, known as the biryani capital of India. Every dish is prepared using time-honored recipes passed down through generations, combined with the freshest local ingredients.

We believe in the perfect balance of spices, the finest quality meats and vegetables, and cooking techniques that bring out the best in every grain of rice. Whether you're a biryani connoisseur or trying it for the first time, we promise an unforgettable culinary experience.
